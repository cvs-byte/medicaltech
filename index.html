FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our clinic management software India and pharmacy systems.

How does the Medicares doctor appointment system work?

Our doctor appointment system provides a complete solution for online appointment booking for doctors. Clinics can configure consultation slots, accept online patient reservations, coordinate with walk-ins at the front-desk, and send automated email and SMS notification schedules to reduce no-shows.

Does the clinic management software India support GST billing?

Yes. Medicares is fully optimized for Indian businesses. The integrated medical billing software India handles custom OPD charges, prescription items, and generates GST-compliant bills automatically calculating CGST and SGST rates based on the relevant drug HSN codes.

Can retail chemists use Medicares as a pharmacy inventory software?

Absolutely. Medicares contains a robust pharmacy management system with GST that operates as real-time pharmacy inventory software. It allows pharmacy owners to record drug batches, monitor stock quantities, set reorder levels, and receive automated warnings for expiring medicines.

How does the digital prescription system benefit clinics and pharmacies?

The digital prescription system allows doctors to write prescriptions electronically on tablets or laptops. The prescriptions are immediately saved in the doctor patient management system database. The in-house or partner pharmacy can pull up the patient details instantly, minimizing manual entry errors and dispensing wait times.

Is Medicares suitable as appointment scheduling software for clinics in India?

Yes, it is specifically customized for clinics, individual practitioners, and pharmacies. The tool acts as an appointment scheduling software for clinics, connecting patient appointments, digital history tracking, pharmacy transactions, and tax reporting under one dashboard.

How can I book doctor appointments online on Medicares?

Our doctor appointment booking online system makes scheduling quick and easy. Patients can find verified doctors, check live calendar availability, and book doctor online India slots instantly without waiting.

What operations are included in the hospital management system?

The digital hospital management system helps clinics and small hospitals manage patient records, slot bookings, staff schedules, and billing streams securely under a unified dashboard.

Does the platform provide pharmacy management software?

Yes. Medicares integrates a powerful pharmacy management software package to help retail chemists track medicine inventory, manage supplier details, verify drug expiries, and issue GST tax receipts.

Is there an online consultation platform available?

Medicares operates as an integrated online consultation platform where doctors can write and issue e-prescriptions, track compliance logs, and sync details seamlessly with in-house pharmacy systems.

How does Medicares secure data in its healthcare management system?

Our healthcare management system uses encrypted JWT (JSON Web Tokens) and role-based access protocols to secure patient histories, prescription sheets, and hospital records against unauthorized leaks.
